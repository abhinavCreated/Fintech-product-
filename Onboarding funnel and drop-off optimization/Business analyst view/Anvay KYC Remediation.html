Decision

    Metric this movesKYC completion ratebaseline 33%, driven by the Aadhaar step

    Three reasons people left, and what answers each

    Trust
    "Why do you need a photo of my Aadhaar?"

    The old screen never says. Users who hesitate leave without ever seeing an error.

    Answered by

    A plain list of what we take and what we don't, shown before the consent tick. Aadhaar appears masked on every screen after.

    Technical
    "It keeps saying the image isn't clear."

    Quality is checked after upload, with no reason given. Each retry repeats the whole step.

    Answered by

    DigiLocker fetch removes the photo for most users. The photo fallback guides the capture before it fails.

    Technical
    "The code hasn't come."

    One channel, one timer. A slow SMS ends the session before KYC even starts.

    Answered by

    The code is read from the SMS when possible, and a call is offered when it isn't.

    • The testSplit new sign-ups 50/50 between the old and new flow. Run full weeks so weekday and weekend behaviour both count.
    • Read the exits by causeTag every exit: left on the consent screen (trust), left at the DigiLocker handoff or photo step (technical). The old funnel can't tell these apart.
    • GuardrailsDetails-mismatch rate on the review screen must not rise. Consent must be recorded for 100% of fetches.

    Requirement IDs used above

    IDRequirement
    FR-01Show the four steps and name the ID step on the first screen.
    FR-02Read the OTP from the SMS when possible. Offer a call when the SMS is late.
    FR-03Fetch identity details from DigiLocker. No Aadhaar photo in the main path.
    FR-04Show fetched details for confirmation. Wrong details route to the photo path.
    FR-05Photo fallback gives live guidance before capture and rejects with a reason.
    REG-01Record explicit consent, with a timestamp, before any identity data is fetched.
    REG-02Never display the Aadhaar number in full. Show it masked.

    Figures come from my simulated 10,000-user funnel, not a live product. Anvay Money is a made-up brand and the DigiLocker screen is a placeholder. The IDs match the BRD and FRD in my KYC Requirements Pack. Regulatory wording would be confirmed with a compliance team before build.
